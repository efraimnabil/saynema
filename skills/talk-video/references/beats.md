# beats.md — the ideas on the prompter

The prompter shows one idea at a time, big, right under the camera, so the
eyes stay near the lens. It is a reminder, not a script: the user says it in
their own words.

## Format

A markdown table; the recorder reads only the rows that start with a number.

```markdown
# <title>

| # | idea | hint |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | 6 months ago, teachers dropped the notebook | the hook: say it like news |
| 2 | Who I am, in one line | name + what you built |
| 3 | The pain: attendance on paper | ask it as a question |
| 4 | What the app does about it | one example, not a list |
| 5 | Money: who paid, who owes | |
| 6 | Proof: 700+ students on it | their real number |
| 7 | The offer | first month free, then the price |
| 8 | What to do now | link in the first comment |
```

- **idea**: 3–8 words. The last word is highlighted on the prompter, so end
  on the key word.
- **hint**: optional, small, under the idea: tone, or what to remember.

## What makes good ideas

- 6–10 ideas for a 40–70 second video. One idea ≈ one or two sentences.
- **First idea = the hook**: a surprising fact, a question, a result. Not
  "hi, my name is".
- One idea per row. If a row has "and", it's probably two.
- Put the user's real facts in the hints (numbers, names) so they don't
  have to remember them, and so you can check the widgets against them later.
- **Last idea = one call to action.** Just one.
- Write in the language they will speak, in their register (dialect is fine).
